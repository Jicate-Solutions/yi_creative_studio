🔐 WHAT IS SUPER ADMIN ACCESS?

Super Admin = Platform-level access to manage ALL organizations, users, credits, and system settings

You can now:
• View and manage all users across platform
• Create/edit/manage all organizations
• Allocate credits to any organization
• View platform analytics and revenue
• Access audit logs

• And much more...

Step 1: LOGOUT from your current session
