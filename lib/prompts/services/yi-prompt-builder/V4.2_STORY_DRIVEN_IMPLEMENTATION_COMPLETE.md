### ✅ Phase 2: Design Intelligence AI Prompt Enhancement

**File**: `lib/prompts/services/design-intelligence.ts` (Lines 313-636)

**Major Enhancement**: Completely rewrote AI prompt with **7-Step Story-Driven Design Framework**:

#### Step 1: Story Analysis
- Identify core narrative, emotional arc, characters
- Extract primary message, secondary themes, transformation
- Analyze formality, energy level, time horizon, scope

#### Step 2: Vibe & Mood Discovery
- Determine 3-5 vibe keywords (empowering, prestigious, urgent, playful, etc.)
- Define mood atmosphere
- Identify emotional temperature and energy dynamics

#### Step 3: Typography Storytelling
- Design headline personality
- **Multi-Color Typography Strategy**: Analyze which words carry POWER, ACTION, EMOTION
- Example: "CLIMATE" (green) "LEADERSHIP" (gold) "SUMMIT" (blue)

#### Step 4: Color Storytelling
- Determine dominant hues based on story themes
- Apply color psychology (leadership→gold, environment→green, tech→blue)
- Define multi-color rhythm

#### Step 5: Background & Atmosphere
- Choose background treatment (linear, radial, sunburst, mesh, atmospheric)
- Specify exact gradient with color stops and direction
- Define atmospheric effects (depth, light rays, particles)

#### Step 6: Decorative Elements
- Generate story-specific thematic elements with placement and reasoning
- Example: "Refined geometric leaf patterns (NOT cartoon leaves)" for Climate + Leadership
- Set sophistication level

#### Step 7: Layout Narrative Flow
- Define visual hierarchy for storytelling
- Design content area styling
- Determine logo strip treatment
- Set spatial density

export function buildEventDetailsCardContext(
  cardConfig?: EventDetailsCardConfig,

  // Extract vibe, sophistication, and energy from Design Intelligence

  const sophisticationLevel = designContext?.decorativeElementsContext?.sophisticationLevel || 'balanced'
  const energyLevel = designContext?.vibeAndMood?.energyDynamics || 'static'
  const emotionalTemp = designContext?.vibeAndMood?.emotionalTemperature || 'neutral'

  // Dynamic border-radius based on sophistication

    sophisticationLevel === 'refined' ? 16 :
    sophisticationLevel === 'playful' ? 24 :

Story Context: ${storyContext}

This styling was chosen to match the event's ${vibe} vibe and ${sophisticationLevel} sophistication level.
`
}
